About the artwork

The Reliquary of Avatar examines identity as a mutable construction shaped by digital systems of representation. Three distinct avatars present variations of a single subject, revealing how appearance, classification, and mediated perception influence the way identity is understood. By shifting between recognizable and stylized forms, the work questions whether digital representations express an existing self or participate in producing it.
