About the artwork

The Reliquary of Intimacy explores how mediated environments affect the perception and experience of human connection. Two deliberately ambiguous digital figures approach an embrace, moving from hesitation toward mutual acceptance. By withholding stable identities, the work directs attention to gesture, vulnerability, and emotional recognition, questioning whether the authenticity of an intimate experience depends on the physical or digital origin of those involved.

If the feeling is real, does its origin matter?
